Making Boring Stuff Exciting Since Day 1 ✨

I'm an Imagineer — an experience designer who borrows the tricks of theme-park storytelling and gives them to AI and spatial computing. Dashboards, operating systems, workflows, arcade cabinets: if it's a little dull on paper, I'd like to turn it into a place you want to walk through.

My process hasn't changed since the first sketchbook: sketch it in pencil, blueprint the magic, prototype with AI, then ship it to the cloud. I run JAM Studios between Los Angeles, Orlando and Guatapé, and I write and draw along the way — author, artist, entrepreneur.

Outside Work…

Most mornings start under El Peñol, the giant rock above Guatapé, Colombia — my home base. It's 740 steps to the top, and the view of the lake is the best brainstorm I know.

The town is painted in every colour a set designer could wish for. Living inside it keeps reminding me that the best worlds start with a feeling, not a feature list.

Ideas used to wait weeks for a model shop. Now I sketch in pencil over breakfast, blueprint the magic by lunch and have AI prototype a walk-through before sunset. AI is the fastest apprentice in the shop — the story decisions stay mine.
