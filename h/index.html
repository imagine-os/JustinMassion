[ About ]

An Imagineer who treats wonder as infrastructure — not decoration.

“

Hi, I'm Justin. I build worlds that feel like they were dreamed up in a theme-park back room, then shipped to the cloud. My foundation is Imagineering: turning a story into a place you can walk through. Every project starts as a pencil sketch, becomes a blueprint, gets prototyped with AI and ships where people can actually touch it — a chameleon with opinions, arcade cabinets drawn like rides, an operating system floating in the sky.

Home base is Guatapé, Colombia, under El Peñol, 740 steps above the lake, with work in Los Angeles and Orlando. Imagineering is 20% blueprint and 80% magic. The trick is making the magic still work on Monday morning.

© How a world gets built プロセス[Pro-cess®]Method

Pro-cess®

Four steps, every time. The order never changes; the medium does — pencil, ink, AI, cloud.

01 —

Sketch

Sketch it in pencil. Every world starts as a line in a notebook: fast, cheap and honest about what the idea really is.

PencilOutput: sketchnotes
02 —

Blueprint

Blueprint the magic. Rides, cabinets and rooms get drawn to scale — sightlines, queues, reveals — before anything gets built.

InkOutput: blueprints
03 —

Prototype

Prototype with AI. Characters, agents and interfaces come alive fast enough to walk through, poke at and rewrite.

AIOutput: working prototypes
04 —

Ship

Ship to the cloud. The world goes live where people can actually touch it — and say "buenos días" back.

CloudOutput: live worlds
